The workshop

No particleboard. No staples. No shortcuts.

Every frame is joined with mortise-and-tenon or dovetail joinery — the same methods cabinetmakers have used for three hundred years, because nothing built since works better. We mill our own wood, cut every joint by hand, and stand behind the frame for thirty years.

01

Wood is kiln-dried on site, then rested for 14 days before milling.

02

Joints are cut and fitted by hand — no fasteners, no adhesive shortcuts.

03

Each piece is signed and dated by the maker before it ships.
